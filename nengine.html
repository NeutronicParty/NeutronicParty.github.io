Privacy

Opting out of logging.

If you would like your data not to be logged, join the Discord server and open a ticket to opt out. Please never be impatient — you will almost always (99% of the time) get a response in your ticket.

When you opt out, no data except for key usage will be logged. Keys are entirely free and never require tasks like off-site links, ads, or promotional content.

For the exact list of what is logged (IP, Time-Zone, UserAgent — and no more), plus the full policy, see the Info page.
