How do I install neutronic engine?

Follow the steps below to get Neutronic Engine running.

To install Neutronic Engine, go to tampermonkey.net and get the tampermonkey extension. After that in your browser go to your manage extensions section, if you do not know how to do this please search it up. Though usually you just click the jigsaw/extensions button in the top right of your browsers topbar and click manage extensions. Then if tampermonkey is in your extensions click on the details button, then toggle ON the "allow userscripts" toggle. This is required for the script to work.
